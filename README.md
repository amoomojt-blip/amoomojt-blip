### 🔍 Project Context
Mining work environments demand strict equipment safety monitoring. Traditional logging mechanisms frequently cause accountability gaps and delayed risk reporting. **Group6-ChecklistApp** modernizes this environment by digitizing pre-shift hazard checks into a live-sync system for site supervisors.

### 👥 My Team Role & Contributions
While engineering team members managed frontend code components and backend architecture, my core focus was guiding the operational usability, team tracking, and product alignment:
*   **Requirement Specification:** Helped translate regulatory mining safety requirements into clear, mobile-optimized checklist step flows.
*   **User Flow Review:** Validated user-journey paths to ensure field technicians could log critical safety information quickly and without confusion before starting their shifts.
*   **Quality & Coordination:** Tracked team progress metrics against our project roadmap milestones to ensure the group stayed organized and aligned.
